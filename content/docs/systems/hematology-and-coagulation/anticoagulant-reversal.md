---
title: Anticoagulant Reversal
weight: 30
---

In patients with life-threatening bleeding or requiring emergency surgery, withhold anticoagulation and consider reversal according to the agent, time since last dose, and bleeding severity.

## Overview


| Anticoagulant | Reversal agent | Important considerations |
|:---:|:---:|---|
| **UFH** | Protamine sulfate | 1 mg per 100 units of UFH administered recently<br>Maximum 50 mg per dose<br>Administer over 10 mins |
| **LMWH**<br>(enoxaparin) | Protamine sulfate | Partial reversal (~60% of anti-Xa activity)<br>1 mg per 1 mg enoxaparin if administered within 8 h |
| **Warfarin** | IV vitamin K<br>+<br>4-factor PCC (Beriplex) | Vitamin K 5–10 mg IV<br>4F-PCC provides rapid reversal<br>Vitamin K provides sustained reversal |
| **Dabigatran** | Idarucizumab | 5 g IV (2 × 2.5 g)<br>Rapid, specific reversal<br>Administer over 10 mins |
| **Factor Xa inhibitors**<br>(apixaban, rivaroxaban, edoxaban) | 4-factor PCC (Beriplex) | Not a true reversal agent<br>Consider 25–50 units/kg (max 3000 units) IV for life-threatening bleeding<br>Andexanet alfa is an agent-specific alternative, but is unavailable in NZ |
