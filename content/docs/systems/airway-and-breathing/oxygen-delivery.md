## Overview
Oxygen delivery devices can be classified into **variable performance** and **fixed performance devices**.

| Class | Device | Flow (L/min) | Approx. FiO₂ | General indications |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Variable** | Nasal cannula | 1–6 | 24–44% | Stable patients, low O₂ requirement |
| **Variable** | Simple face mask (Hudson) | 5–12 | 35–65% | Cheap, moderate O₂ requirement |
| **Variable** | Non-rebreather (reservoir) | 10–15 | 60–90% | Emergency, critical illness, pre-oxygenation |
| **Fixed** | Venturi mask | 2–15 | 24–60% | Hypercapnia risk (COPD) |
| **Fixed** | High-flow nasal cannula | 20–60 | 21–100% | Type 1 respiratory failure, ↑ work of breathing, dead space washout |
| **Fixed** | CPAP | Variable | 21–100% | Cardiogenic pulmonary edema, OSA |
| **Fixed** | BiPAP / NIV | Variable | 21–100% | Type 2 respiratory failure, COPD exacerbation |
| **Fixed** | Invasive Mechanical Ventilator | Variable | 21–100% | Airway protection, failed HFNO/NIV |
