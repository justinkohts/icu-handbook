## Sepsis and Septic Shock
### Sepsis
**Sepsis** is the life-threatening organ dysfunction caused by a dysregulated host response to infection. This organ function is identified as an acute change in **SOFA score ≥ 2 points** attributable to infection. The SOFA score assesses **organ dysfunction** across six organ systems: respiratory, cardiovascular, renal, liver, coagulation, neurologic.

### Septic Shock
**Septic shock** is a subset of sepsis characterized by profound circulatory and cellular/metabolic dysfunction:
- Vasopressor requirement to maintain MAP > 65 mmHg.
- Serum lactate > 2 mmol/L in the absence of hypovolemia.

## Screening and Recognition
**Screening:**
- Use the [NZEWS (New Zealand Early Warning Scores)](https://www.clinicalguidance.org.nz/guidelines/new-zealand-early-warning-scores-nzews) for screening acutely ill patients.
- The SOFA score quantifies organ dysfunction but is not intended as an early screening tool.

**Clinical Recognition:**
- Sepsis is a clinical diagnosis. No single biomarker or test can confirm or exclude it.
- Suspect sepsis in patients with infection and new organ dysfunction (e.g. hypoxemia, hypotension, altered mental status, oliguria).

**Initial Investigations:**
- Blood cultures ASAP, ideally before antibiotics, without delaying treatment.
- Serum lactate.
- Other investigations directed toward identifying the source of infection and assessing organ dysfunction.


## Management
**Infection management:**
- **Empiric antimicrobial therapy** according to suspected source.
- **Source control:** identify sources requiring drainage, debridement, surgery, removal of infected devices.

**Hemodynamic resuscitation:**
- **Fluid resuscitation** with IV crystalloids.
- **Vasopressors.**
- **Perfusion monitoring** with capillary refill time, mentation, urine output, serum lactate, and other clinical perfusion parameters.

**Other organ support and adjuncts:**
- **Respiratory:** supplemental O₂, ventilatory support.
- **Corticosteroids** in septic shock.
- **Renal:** CRRT, SLEDD.
- **Other supportive care:** glycemic control, VTE prophylaxis, early enteral nutrition.
