## ICU Data Sheets

Waikato ICU contributes to the [ANZICS (Australian & NZ Intensive Care Society)](https://www.anzics.org/advocacy/core-icu-registries/registry-datasets/) database. Every patient admitted to ICU must have a datasheet completed.  The majority of information required is to be filled in by the admitting registrar, however the completion of the form is the responsibility of the discharging registrar.    

The collection of this data is a requirement of CICM and enables us to benchmark the quality of our care against other Australasian units.  This ensures we are maintaining appropriate standards and clinical practice in the unit. The data can also be utilised for audit and research projects ranging from small local projects all the way up to the large ANZICS Clinical Trials.


## Clinical Audits
Registrars are encouraged to do a clinical audit project during each run. These may be presented at regional meetings or Grand Rounds.
