Clinical documentation is an important part of patient care and forms part of the legal medical record. Notes should be clear, accurate, and contemporaneous.

There are a few types of clinical notes the ICU Registar may utilize.


## Handover Sheet

- Exists as a computerized handover sheet in MS Teams.
- This is updated multiple times daily to assist patient handovers.


## Admission Note

Use the relevant admission proforma where available, e.g. Cardiac and Trauma admissions. A generic admission template exists in the admission note archives on the shared desktop.

In general, these capture the following bits of information:
- Date and time.
- Admitting RMO and SMO.
- Reason for ICU admission.
- Relevant history and current medications.
- Examination findings.
- Important investigation results.
- Clinical assessment, illness severity, and differential diagnosis.
- Management plan.

This is not only important for clinical use, but also research and audit purposes.


## Wardround Note
Use the ICU daily review template, and document:
- Date and time.
- Intensivist leading the wardround.
- Relevant organ system assessment and current ICU support.
- Clinical impression.
- Management plan.

Ensure the note is correctly identified with the patient's name and hospital number, and is filed in the clinical record promptly.


## Outreach/Referral Review Note
As described in [Referrals & Outreach]({{< ref "referrals-outreach.md#documentation" >}}), document the reason for referral, assessment, discussions, agreed management plan, disposition, and any planned ICU follow-up.

Again, notes should be clear, accurate, and contemporaneous.
