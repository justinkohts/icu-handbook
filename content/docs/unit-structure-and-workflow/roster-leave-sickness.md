## Leave
There are many types of leave including annual leave, conference leave, professional leave (for courses), study leave, and sick leave.

Please let us know of any leave requests well in advance. These can usually be arranged at times requested, providing enough notice is given, e.g. several months.

These will normally be covered by the Reliever registrar.


## Sickness
If you are sick for a rostered shift, you must ring/text as early as possible:
- Mon–Fri: Daytime Consultant
- After hours: On Call Consultant
