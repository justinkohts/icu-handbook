## Information Technology

Computers throughout the ICU provide access to the hospital network, clinical systems, laboratory results, and other patient information.

The hospital intranet provides access to:
- Clinical policies, guidelines, and critical care drug protocols.
- Library services and online journals.
- Staff directories and other hospital resources.

You will be provided with an **individual network login and email account** by the IT department. Do not share your login details or leave clinical systems unattended while logged in.


**Internet access** is available through the hospital network. Some websites may be restricted for security reasons. If access to a clinically relevant resources is blocked, contact the IT service desk to request access.



## Medicolegal Assistance
If you require medicolegal advice, please contact the hospital's legal advisor via the switchboard.

You may occasionally be asked to provide a report for an internal investigation, the Coronial Service, the Health and Disability Commissioner, or another external agency.

Reports should generally be prepared with the oversight of a Senior Medical Officer, particularly where they relate to patient care or a significant clinical event.

If you are unsure who to approach, or would prefer not to approach the SMO involved in the case, seek advice from the ICU Director, Deputy Director, or Supervisor of Training.
