## Structure of a Regular Work Day

| Time | Activity | Duration |
|:---:|---|:---:|
| **08:00** | Morning handover | 30–45 min |
| **09:00** | Consultant-led bedside ward round | — |
| **11:30** | Handover room — SMO, RMO, and ACNM catch-up. Radiology or ID meeting. | 15–30 min |
| **16:00** | ICU handover between Cardiac and Main Unit consultants | 30 min |
| **20:00** | Evening handover and HDU review | 60 min |

## Teaching and Regular ICU Meetings
| Activity | Day | Time | Venue |
|---|---|:---:|:---:|
| **Registrar Teaching** | Tuesday afternoons | 13:00–15:30 | ICU meeting room |
| **ICU Microbiology/ID Meeting** | Mondays and Thursdays | 11:30 | ICU meeting room |
| **Radiology Meeting** | Wednesdays and weekends | 11:30 | ICU meeting room |
| **Mortality & Morbidity (M&M)** | Typically once every quarter on Tuesday | 13:00–15:30 in place of Registrar Teaching session | ICU meeting room |
