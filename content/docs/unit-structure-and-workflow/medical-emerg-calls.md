There are 3 types of medical emergency calls, all of which are transmitted by pager.



## Cardiac Arrest Calls
The cardiac arrest team members comprise of:
- ICU Registrar
- Cardiology or Medical Registrar, according to time of day
- Nurses: CCU nurse, PAR nurses, ward staff


### CPR (Cardiopulmonary Resuscitation)
Waikato Hospital encourages the use of the [International Liaison Committee on Resuscitation (ILCOR)](https://ilcor.org/) guidelines for CPR.

In general, the **ICU Registrar** is responsible for supporting the airway and establishing effective ventilation, whilst the **Medical Registrar** should manage the cardiac and general aspects of patient management. However, it would be expected that the **more senior Registrar** present should be directing advanced life support.

Additional support is available from:
- ICU SMO, staff, and equipment.
- The two Anesthesia Registrars rostered onsite overnight.
- ACNMs and PAR nurses, who are often capable at airway support also.

All ICU involvement in cardiac arrest calls must be documented in the patient record.


## MET (Medical Emergency Team) Calls
ICU Registrars may be called to MET call activations when a patient is significantly deteriorating or may require intensive care support.

The ICU Registrar should:
- Rapidly assess the patient, assist with stabilization, and treat immediate life threats.
- Communicate with the team to formulate a management plan.
- Assess whether ICU admission or further ICU is needed.
- Escalate early to the Intensivist when required.


## Trauma Calls
The trauma call usually precedes the patient arrival in ED. The team assesmbled will vary according to the number of cases expected.

Before arrival:
- Confirm roles with the trauma team.
- Prepare and draw up likely medications.
- Check airway and intubation equipment.
- Identify the airway assistant.

For patients with life-threatening injuries and suspected major hemorrhage, the **Trauma Code Red protocol** provides a structured approach to early time-critical management, and is available under **Policies & Guidelines** via the hospital intranet.

The ICU registrar's role is to **assist with patient management** and **plan for ICU admission** where appropriate. Keep ICU senior medical and nursing staff up to date with patient progress if ICU admission is likely.
