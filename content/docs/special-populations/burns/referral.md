## Referral to Middlemore Hospital
Severe burn management has become increasingly specialized over time. Waikato Hospital is designated as one of the four Regional Burns Units in New Zealand by [ANZBA](https://www.anzba.org/) (Australian and New Zealand Burns Association). The **National Burn Center** is located at **Middlemore Hospital**, Auckland.

### Criteria for referral to Middlemore Hospital
- Burns > 30% of total body surface area (TBSA).
- Full thickness burns to face, hands, genitals, or perineum.
- Burn injury with significant inhalation injury.
- High voltage electrical burns.
- Significant chemical burns.

### How to Refer
1. Complete the [referral form](https://www.healthnz.govt.nz/health-professionals/guidance-standards/topic/burns/national-burn-service#NBCreferralform)
2. Email completed form to oncallburnsnurse@middlemore.co.nz
3. Send images of cleaned wounds to oncallburnsnurse@middlemore.co.nz
4. Phone the on-call burns coordinator at 092503800 to confirm receipt of forms & images

**Do not** call the Middlemore Burn surgeon or ICU directly.
